## Research

My master's thesis work at the Institute of Biological Physics involves physics-informed wet lab experiments to model and simulate biological systems.

The work combines molecular biology lab techniques with computational analysis and simulation.

## Methods & tools

- Physics-informed experimentation
- Molecular biology lab techniques
- Data analysis with NumPy and SciPy
- Modeling with PyTorch

## Context

This research forms part of my MSc in Computational Sciences at the University of Cologne, where I specialize in computational biology.
