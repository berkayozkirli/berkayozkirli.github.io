## Research

Under the supervision of Prof. Emre Akbaş, I built foundation models for histopathology using self-supervised learning, specifically **DINO**.

I fine-tuned these models for medical image segmentation, connecting representation learning with downstream analysis of tissue images.

## Methods & tools

- Self-supervised learning with DINO
- Foundation models for histopathology
- Fine-tuning for medical image segmentation
- Python, PyTorch, and Amazon Web Services
